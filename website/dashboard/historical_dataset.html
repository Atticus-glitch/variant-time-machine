Safety Rules

  • The safe limit is the smallest of 5 GB, 10% of currently free space, and the amount that leaves at least 20 GB free.
  • A wrong-size file, symbolic link, directory, or other unsafe existing target blocks the pair.
  • The approval token is valid only for the exact server-issued plan and can be used once.
  • The builder never deletes an existing release automatically and never extracts a full archive to disk.
