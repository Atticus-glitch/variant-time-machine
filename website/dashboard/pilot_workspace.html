Quick help

What These Words Mean

VUS: A variant of uncertain significance does not yet have enough evidence for a clear classification.

Current is not past: Today’s ClinVar page cannot prove what ClinVar showed on an older date.

Verified: Every required identifier, date, category, source, and checklist item was checked by a person.

Sources matter: Another researcher needs the exact official link to repeat the check.

Categories stay separate: Protective, drug response, oncogenic, benign, and pathogenic describe different kinds of claims.
