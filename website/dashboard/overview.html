Important limits

What This Does Not Prove

  • A changed aggregate classification is not automatically a confirmed medical reclassification.
  • LastEvaluated is a ClinVar source field; it is not the release date or download date.
  • The broad spreadsheet contains two snapshots only. Intermediate changes require exact VCV history review.
  • Condition scope, merged records, complex alleles, and conflicting submissions can make an apparent change ambiguous.
  • This research site is not a medical device and must not be used for clinical decisions.
