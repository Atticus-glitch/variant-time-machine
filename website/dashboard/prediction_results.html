    On these same records, frozen V7 reached 86.6688% balanced accuracy. V8's +0.4524-point difference had a component-bootstrap interval of -2.45 to +3.31 points, so it did not establish overall superiority. Missense balanced accuracy was 63.82% versus 55.88% on 230 records, but that paired interval also included zero.

    This is membership-hidden retrospective evidence, not a never-opened future test. July 2026 was already accessed for V7 and membership is reconstructible. Preserved weighting, tie-break, and out-of-fold reuse caveats qualify the implementation.
