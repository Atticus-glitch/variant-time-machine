Why Original V9 Scored Lower

Original V9 did not receive cleaner labels or a harder new cohort. It relearned from only the 1,000 opened records, while V8 had used a 9,818-record development matrix. Its regularization, weighting, and low thresholds shifted predictions toward pathogenic calls: pathogenic recall increased, but false positives rose to 113 and accuracy fell to 86%.

V9.1 addresses the data-volume and selection issues by adding the authenticated V8 development matrix inside each outer training partition and nesting every family, configuration, calibration, and threshold choice. It does not repair the absence of clean reviewed labels or an untouched final test.
