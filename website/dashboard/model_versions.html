What Stayed Frozen With V8

Effective fitting weights were not strictly equal per component; the simplicity tie-break did not rank regularization strength within the logistic family; and grouped out-of-fold labels were reused for final candidate selection, calibration, and threshold choice. These caveats qualify the development claim without changing the sealed result.
