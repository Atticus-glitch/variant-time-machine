## ClinVar

- Intended role: historical and newer variant classifications, submission evidence, review status, and stable identifiers.
- Source to verify: official NCBI ClinVar archive and documentation.
- Research needed: archived formats, release frequency, identifier stability, classification terminology, and conditions attached to classifications.

## gnomAD

- Intended possible role: population allele frequencies and gene-constraint information available at a historical cutoff.
- Source to verify: official gnomAD releases and documentation.
- Research needed: release dates, population fields, genome builds, missingness, licensing or citation requirements, and historical availability.

## UniProt

- Intended possible role: protein function, domains, and mapped regions.
- Source to verify: official UniProt releases and documentation.
- Research needed: archived annotations, version dates, identifier mapping, evidence levels, and terms of use.
