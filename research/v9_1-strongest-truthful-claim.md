# V9.1 strongest truthful claim

The fully nested V9.1 internal-development procedure reached component-weighted balanced accuracy 0.8939 on the 1,000 previously opened V8 records, compared with 0.8735 for original V9 and 0.8822 for frozen V8 on those same records. Its pathogenic recall was 0.8978. The paired 95% intervals for the primary-metric differences crossed zero against both references, and V9.1 used labels that were sealed for V8, so these results do not establish superiority over either model.

The fitted V9.1 ExtraTrees artifact is an internal candidate only. It has no independent final-test score, no clean reviewed-cohort result, no demonstrated clinical validity, and no basis for deployment or clinical use.
